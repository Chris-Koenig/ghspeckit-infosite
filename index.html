Why SpecKit Now

Developers thrive when they can think, build, and iterate without friction. Spec Kit removes the guesswork by giving AI agents and humans the same context. No more reverse-engineering intentions from scattered commits and chat threads.

Specs become part of the workflow — versioned, reviewable, and always close to code. Developers can explore new ideas safely, knowing the "why" behind every decision is documented. Every PR, branch, and experiment stays anchored to an evolving source of truth.

Spec Kit turns context into a developer superpower — a clear, shared, and adaptable foundation that makes building with AI not just faster, but genuinely smarter.
